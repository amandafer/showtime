# ShowTime

A personal TV Time-style tracker for series and films.

- **Up next**: your next unwatched episode for every show, with one-tap ✓
- **Episode checklists** per season, with "mark earlier episodes too"
- **Coming soon**: air dates for shows you follow
- **Search** series (TVmaze) and films (Wikipedia), with posters
- **Netflix import** from your viewing history CSV
- Works offline and installs to your phone's home screen

Data is stored on your device (localStorage). Use *More → Save backup* to keep a copy.

TV data © [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com) (CC BY-SA). Film data from Wikipedia.
